RadiX Dose

A reference absorbed-dose calculator for external-beam radiotherapy.

iPhone · iPad · Mac

Coming to the App Store

RadiX Dose turns an ionization-chamber reading into a verified absorbed dose to water — under IAEA TRS-398 and AAPM TG-51 — and keeps every calculation, with the equipment and calibrations behind it, in one auditable place. Built by a medical physicist, for medical physicists.

Release-ready

Reference Dosimetry

Compute absorbed dose to water from a chamber reading under IAEA TRS-398 or AAPM TG-51, check output against expected, and save an auditable record — with live environmental telemetry, a full equipment library, and one-tap reporting.

In development

TPS Commissioning

Point-dose validation of a treatment-planning system against measurement, per IAEA TRS-430 — reusing the same verified dose engine. A working preview, not yet a validated clinical tool.
